FAQ

Local SEO Questions Answered

What is the difference between local SEO and regular SEO?

Regular SEO focuses on improving your visibility in general search results, while Local SEO specifically targets location-based searches. Local SEO includes optimizing your Google Business Profile, building local citations, and targeting geographic keywords — elements that specifically improve your visibility to customers searching in your area.

How long does local SEO take to see results in Albuquerque?

Local SEO results typically appear within 2 to 4 months for Google Business Profile improvements, and 3 to 6 months for broader local search rankings. The timeline depends on your current local search presence, the competitiveness of your market, and the scope of the work being done. Consistent effort over time produces compounding results.

Do I need a Google Business Profile to rank in local search?

Yes. A verified and fully optimized Google Business Profile is essential for appearing in Google Maps results and the local search pack. Without it, your business will have very limited visibility in location-based searches. If you haven't yet claimed your profile, that's often the first priority.

Can you help businesses outside Albuquerque?

Yes. While we are based in Albuquerque, New Mexico, we provide local SEO and broader SEO services to businesses throughout New Mexico and across the United States. Our strategies are adaptable to any local market.
