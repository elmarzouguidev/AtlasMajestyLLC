Service 02

Technical SEO

Technical SEO ensures that search engines can effectively crawl, understand, and index your website. Even the best content will underperform if technical issues prevent Google from properly reading your pages.

We conduct thorough technical audits and implement improvements across site architecture, crawlability, indexation, page speed, Core Web Vitals, structured data, and more.

  • Crawlability and indexation analysis
  • Core Web Vitals optimization
  • Site architecture and URL structure
  • Structured data / Schema.org markup
  • Mobile usability and responsiveness
  • Page speed and performance improvements

Service 03

On-Page SEO

On-Page SEO is the process of optimizing individual web pages so they rank higher for targeted search queries and attract the right visitors. It ensures every page on your website is optimized to communicate relevance and value to both search engines and real users.

  • Keyword research and targeting
  • Title tags and meta descriptions
  • Heading structure (H1, H2, H3)
  • Content optimization for search intent
  • Internal linking strategy
  • Image optimization and alt text
