How to Choose the Right Keywords for Your SEO Strategy

By Atlas Majesty LLC · Published February 2025 · 6 Min Read

Targeting the wrong keywords is one of the most frequent reasons SEO campaigns fail to generate revenue. Attracting thousands of visitors searching for general definitions won’t grow your business if none of them need your services.

Understanding Search Intent

Every Google search has a goal behind it. Google categorizes search intent into three primary buckets:

  • Informational: The user wants answers or tutorials (e.g., "what is on-page SEO").
  • Navigational: The user is searching for a specific brand or portal.
  • Transactional / Commercial: The user is comparing providers or ready to buy (e.g., "hire SEO agency Albuquerque" or "local SEO pricing").

Balancing Volume vs. Difficulty

High search volume terms are often fiercely competitive and dominated by massive national directories. For most small to mid-sized businesses, focusing on long-tail keywords and local modifiers produces the fastest path to qualified leads.

Mapping Keywords to Your Website Architecture

Each page on your website should target a primary keyword theme with related secondary variations. Avoid keyword cannibalization (multiple pages competing for the exact same term), and ensure your primary service pages target transactional search intent.
