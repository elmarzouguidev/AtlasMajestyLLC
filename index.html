Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions About SEO

Clear answers to common questions about SEO, how it works, and what to expect from an SEO strategy.

What is SEO and why does my business need it?

SEO (Search Engine Optimization) is the process of improving your website so it ranks higher in search engine results pages like Google. When potential customers search for products or services you offer, SEO helps ensure your business appears in front of them. For most businesses, organic search is one of the most cost-effective long-term sources of qualified traffic and leads.

How long does SEO take to show results?

SEO is a long-term strategy. Most businesses begin to see meaningful improvements within 3 to 6 months, though the timeline varies based on factors such as your industry, the competitiveness of your keywords, your current website condition, domain authority, and the scope of the SEO work being done. Sustainable organic growth compounds over time and typically becomes one of your most reliable sources of traffic.

How much do SEO services cost?

SEO pricing depends on several factors, including your current website condition, the competitiveness of your industry, the scope of services required, and your business goals. We don't believe in one-size-fits-all pricing — we prefer to first understand your situation and then provide a customized proposal. Contact us or request a free SEO audit to start the conversation.

Can you help my local business rank on Google in Albuquerque?

Yes. Local SEO is one of our core specialties. We help local businesses in Albuquerque and across New Mexico improve their visibility in location-based Google searches and Google Maps results. This includes optimizing your Google Business Profile, building local citations, developing locally relevant content, and strengthening your overall local search presence.

What is a Google Business Profile and why does it matter?

A Google Business Profile (formerly Google My Business) is a free listing that allows your business to appear in Google Maps and local search results. An optimized profile helps your business appear in the Google Maps Pack — the group of local business listings that appear prominently at the top of local search results. It's especially important for businesses that serve customers in a specific geographic area.

Why isn't my website ranking on Google?

There are many reasons a website may not rank well in Google, including technical SEO issues (crawlability, indexing, site speed), insufficient or poorly optimized content, lack of relevant backlinks, weak keyword targeting, competition from more authoritative websites, or a relatively new domain. An SEO audit can identify the specific factors holding your website back and prioritize the most impactful improvements.

What does an SEO audit include?

Our SEO audit covers your website's technical health (crawlability, indexing, site speed, Core Web Vitals), on-page optimization (metadata, headings, keyword targeting), content quality and gaps, local SEO presence, backlink profile, and competitive positioning. The goal is to identify exactly what's limiting your search visibility and what improvements will have the greatest impact. Request your free SEO audit to get started.
